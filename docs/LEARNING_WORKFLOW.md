# Learning Workflow

CodeCraft lessons should follow a predictable learning loop.

1. Introduce the concept with a small example.
2. Show the expected output or behavior.
3. Give the learner a short practice task.
4. Let the learner test their understanding.
5. Provide a concise explanation of the solution.
6. Encourage a follow-up challenge.

The goal is to move learners from reading to active practice as quickly as possible.
